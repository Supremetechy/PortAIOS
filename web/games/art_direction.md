# Art Direction: AIOS Mini-Games

## Style & Mood
- **Visual Style**: Clean "AI Operating System" aesthetic. Glassmorphism, minimalist icons, and high-contrast neon accents (electric blue, cyan, magenta).
- **Palette**: Deep charcoal (#0d0d0d) backgrounds, glowing UI elements, and semi-transparent glass panels.
- **Lighting**: Subtle glows and bloom effects for active elements.

## Key Assets
- **OS Background**: 16:9 minimalist dark tech wallpaper.
- **App Icons**: 1:1 stylized icons for Memory, Shooter, and Poker.
- **Game Elements**:
  - Memory/Poker: Geometric "tech" icons (microchip, gear, wifi, code, etc.) on card faces.
  - Shooter: Sleek top-down neon player ship and red enemy drones.
  - Card Back: Intricate blue geometric circuit pattern.

## Implementation Notes
- Use CSS filters (blur, brightness) for glassmorphism.
- Sprite sheets or individual icons for game entities.
- Ensure high legibility for AIOS-native feel.
