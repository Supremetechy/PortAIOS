# Sound Direction: AIOS Mini-Games

## Music Mood
- **Theme**: Ambient synth-wave / Chill electronic.
- **Energy**: Low-energy for OS/Menus, Medium-energy for Games.
- **Loops**: One main ambient OS loop, one energetic arcade track for the shooter.

## Core SFX
- **UI**: Digital "blip" for navigation, "click" for selection.
- **Memory**: Soft success chime, error thud.
- **Shooter**: High-pitched laser blast, electronic explosion, damage "glitch" sound.
- **Poker**: Digital card shuffle, "hand match" fanfare.

## Playback
- Unlock audio on first click/gesture.
- Global mute toggle in the "OS" taskbar.
