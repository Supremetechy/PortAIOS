💡 Pro Tips

Wake Word: Say "Hey AIOS" or click the microphone button to activate voice control.
Natural Language: Commands are flexible. Say "Click demo", "Press demo", or just "demo" - all work!
Gesture Setup: Click the gesture help button (👆) and allow camera access for gesture control.
Visual Feedback: Look for cyan highlights when voice/gesture commands activate elements.
Create Macros: Say "record macro [name]", execute commands, then say "stop recording" to save.
Sensitivity: Adjust gesture sensitivity in settings if detection is too sensitive or not sensitive enough.
Command List: Type window.AIOS.uiVoiceGestureController.getVoiceCommandList() in console for full list.
