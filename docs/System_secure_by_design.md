## Ether to make sure your computer is
secure. Security is very important for protecting your data and
privacy from hackers, malware, and other threats. Here are some tips
to help you keep your computer secure:

Use a firewall: A firewall is a software or hardware that blocks
unauthorized access to your computer from the internet or other
networks. Windows has a built-in firewall that is turned on by
default1. You can check and adjust your firewall settings in Windows
Security2.
Use antivirus software and keep it updated: Antivirus software helps
detect and remove malware, viruses, and other harmful programs from
your computer. Windows Security also includes Microsoft Defender
Antivirus, which provides real-time protection and automatic updates2.
If you have another antivirus program installed, make sure it is
compatible with Windows and update it regularly.
Use strong passwords and protect them: Passwords are the keys to your
online accounts and devices. You should use complex passwords that are
hard to guess and different for each account. You should also avoid
sharing your passwords with anyone or writing them down. You can use a
password manager to store and manage your passwords securely. Windows
Security also offers sign-in options and account settings, such as
Windows Hello and dynamic lock, to help you protect your passwords2.
Be careful with email attachments and links: Email attachments and
links can contain malware or phishing scams that try to steal your
personal information or money. You should not open or click on
anything that looks suspicious or comes from an unknown sender. You
can use Microsoft Defender SmartScreen to help block malicious
websites and downloads2.
Browse the web safely: Some websites can infect your computer with
malware or track your online activity. You should avoid visiting sites
that offer illegal or questionable content, such as pirated movies,
music, or software. You should also use a modern browser like
Microsoft Edge, which has built-in security features to help prevent
malicious code from running on your computer2.
Keep your software up to date: Software updates can fix security
vulnerabilities and improve performance and functionality. You should
turn on automatic updates in Windows Update to keep Windows, Microsoft
Office, and other Microsoft applications up to date1. You should also
update your non-Microsoft software, especially browsers, Adobe Acrobat
Reader, and other apps you regularly use.
Don’t use USBs or other external devices unless you own them: USBs and
other external devices can carry malware or viruses that can infect
your computer when you plug them in. You should only use devices that
belong to you or come from a trusted source. You should also scan them
for viruses before using them.
